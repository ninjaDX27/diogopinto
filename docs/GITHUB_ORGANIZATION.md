## Evidence quality standard

Every featured project must answer: What problem? What evidence? How to run? What works? What does *not* work? Why does this matter for a business decision?

- An offline test is not a live integration.
- Synthetic financial values are not audited business performance.
- A cloned starter is not original authorship.
- No customer data, raw finance documents or credentials in GitHub.
- Do not display invented deployment links or model results.
